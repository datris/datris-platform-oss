## The open-source license

Datris is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/datris/datris-platform-oss/blob/main/LICENSE). In plain terms:

- **Run and modify Datris for any purpose**, including offering it as a hosted service and embedding it in your own product. Your modifications can stay private.
- **If you redistribute Datris**, modified or not, keep the `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files with the copy and mark the files you changed.
- **The name "Datris" is not licensed.** The license covers the code, not the trademark (Apache 2.0 section 6); beyond describing where the code came from, using the name needs separate permission.
- **Contributions are accepted under the same license** (Apache 2.0 section 5). No separate contributor agreement is required.

This is a summary, not legal advice; have your counsel read it against your deployment. The longer treatment, including the questions legal teams usually ask, is at [datris.ai/licensing](https://datris.ai/licensing).

## Commercial support

The software and its license are identical for everyone; a support subscription adds a named technical contact with a response SLA on production incidents, security advisories ahead of public disclosure, an LTS branch with a stated patch window, an SBOM and signed builds, an IP warranty and indemnification, version and migration assistance, and a seat at roadmap discussions. Subscriptions are annual and scoped to your deployment; request a proposal at [datris.ai/licensing](https://datris.ai/licensing).

**Continuity.** The complete source is public under Apache 2.0 today. A Datris deployment never stops running because a vendor did.

## Community support

- **Bugs and feature requests:** [GitHub issues](https://github.com/datris/datris-platform-oss/issues)
- **Questions and design discussions:** [GitHub discussions](https://github.com/datris/datris-platform-oss/discussions)
- **Security reports:** privately, per [Vulnerability Disclosure](/production/vulnerability-disclosure)
- **Advisories and release notes ahead of disclosure:** [register your deployment](https://datris.ai/register) — Datris never calls home, so this is the only way we know you exist.
